# Beaver poses for ALIGN: prompts, one at a time

How to use this:
1. Open your Image Generator Pro chat.
2. Attach `beaver-reference.png` (in this folder) to the first message.
3. Paste ONE prompt below. Wait for the image. Check it against the checklist.
4. If it is good, download it as PNG and upload it in this Claude chat. Then do the next one.
5. If it is wrong, reply in the same ChatGPT chat with the fix line under "If it goes wrong".

Do not ask for more than one pose per message. Keep everything in the same ChatGPT chat so it remembers the character.

## First message (send once, with the reference image attached)

> This is our app mascot: a friendly beaver in a yellow hard hat. Study the attached sheet closely.
> Style rules for every image you make from now on:
> - Flat vector illustration, hand-drawn look, thick dark navy outline (#00224E), about 6% of the head width.
> - Fill colours only: cream body #FFFBF3, hard hat yellow #FFD265, tail tan #E3B987 with a navy cross-hatch, navy #00224E for eyes, nose and lines.
> - Two round navy eyes with a small white shine dot in each, a small navy nose, two front teeth showing, small rosy smile.
> - No gradients, no shadows, no texture, no extra colours, no text.
> - Plain pure white background, character centred, whole character inside the image, nothing cut off.
> - Square image, 1024 x 1024.
> Reply "ready" and wait for my pose requests.

## Animation 1: the peek (head comes up from the bottom edge, waves)

Make these one by one:

**1a. Peek, neutral**
> Pose 1a: only the head and shoulders, seen from the front, as if he is rising up from behind the bottom edge of a phone screen. Paws resting on the edge, eyes open, small smile. Crop at mid-chest, flat horizontal cut at the bottom.

**1b. Peek, wave**
> Pose 1b: exactly the same as 1a, same size and position, but his right paw (our left) is raised and waving, fingers spread, two short motion lines next to the paw. Eyes happy.

**1c. Peek, wink**
> Pose 1c: exactly the same as 1a, same size and position, but he winks with his left eye (our right) and grins wider. Both paws on the edge.

## Animation 2: hanging upside down with a sign (for "no internet" and errors)

**2a. Hanging**
> Pose 2: the beaver hangs upside down by his feet from the top edge of the image. His feet and tail are at the top, partly cut by the top edge. His head is at the bottom, upside down, eyes looking at the viewer, a slightly worried smile. Both arms hang down and hold the top corners of a blank cream sign board (#FFFBF3, thick navy outline, slightly wobbly hand-drawn rectangle). Leave the board empty, no text.

**2b. Hanging, sway**
> Same as Pose 2, same size, but his whole body tilted about 10 degrees to the left, as if swinging, with two short motion lines on the right.

## Animation 3: big head celebration (when a job is closed)

**3a. Big head, cheer**
> Pose 3a: very big head, filling most of the image, cut at the neck at the bottom. Eyes closed in happiness (upside-down U shapes), mouth wide open in a happy shout, three small yellow sparkle stars around the hat.

**3b. Big head, thumbs up**
> Pose 3b: same framing as 3a, eyes open and shining, big smile, one paw coming up from the bottom edge giving a thumbs up.

## Checklist before you download (please look at each one)

- [ ] Two eyes, both drawn, round, same size, each with one white shine dot (or both closed if the pose says so).
- [ ] Hard hat is yellow with a navy rim and the small bump on top, like the reference.
- [ ] Two front teeth showing, nose is a small navy oval.
- [ ] Only the colours listed. No grey shading, no gradient, no drop shadow.
- [ ] Outline is thick and navy everywhere, no missing outline segments.
- [ ] Paws have the right number of fingers (three or four), not six, not merged.
- [ ] Background is plain white. Nothing important is cut off unless the pose asks for it.
- [ ] For a series (1a/1b/1c, 2a/2b, 3a/3b): head size and position match the first image of the series.

## If it goes wrong (reply with one of these)

- "Keep everything the same but fix the eyes: two round navy eyes with one white shine dot each."
- "Remove all shading and gradients. Flat colours only, from the list."
- "Same pose, but match the head size and position of the previous image exactly."
- "The outline is too thin. Make every outline thick navy like the reference."
- "Make the background plain white with nothing else on it."

## What happens after you upload

I check each image (eyes, outline, colours, cut-offs), turn it into a clean vector, set the colours back to the exact app colours, and put the frames into the app as a short one-time animation. If an image fails a check, I'll tell you which fix line to send.
